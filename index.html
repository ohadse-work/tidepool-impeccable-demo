How a reading is made

  1. Wade in to arm's depth.
  2. Hold the thermometer for sixty seconds.
  3. Post it before you towel off.

A reading carries the tower, the minute and the swimmer who took it. Two readings from the same tower within ten minutes are averaged, so one cold hand can't scare off the whole beach.

Read the flag before the number
